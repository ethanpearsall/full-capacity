Set up auto-forwarding in Gmail

1

Open Gmail Settings

Click the gear icon, then "See all settings".

2

Go to "Forwarding and POP/IMAP"

Click the tab at the top of settings.

3

Add a forwarding address

Click "Add a forwarding address" and paste your Full Capacity address. Confirm when prompted.

4

Enable forwarding

Select "Forward a copy of incoming mail to" and choose your Full Capacity address. Keep Gmail's copy in the Inbox.

Set up auto-forwarding in Outlook / Microsoft 365

1

Open Outlook Settings

Click the gear icon, then "View all Outlook settings".

2

Go to Mail, then Forwarding

In the sidebar, click "Mail", then "Forwarding".

3

Enable forwarding

Check "Enable forwarding", paste your Full Capacity address, and check "Keep a copy of forwarded messages".

Set up forwarding with another email provider

Find "Forwarding" or "Rules" in your email provider's settings and add your Full Capacity address as a forwarding destination. We recommend forwarding only emails with attachments.
