Set up auto-forwarding in Gmail

1

Open Gmail Settings

Click the gear icon in the top right of Gmail, then click "See all settings".

2

Go to Forwarding and POP/IMAP

Click the "Forwarding and POP/IMAP" tab at the top of the settings page.

3

Add a forwarding address

Click "Add a forwarding address" and paste your Full Capacity address:

Loading...
4

Confirm the forwarding address

Gmail will send a confirmation code to verify the address. This may take a few minutes. Once received, check your Email dashboard for the confirmation email.

5

Enable forwarding

Go back to Settings, then Forwarding and POP/IMAP. Select "Forward a copy of incoming mail to" and choose your Full Capacity address. We recommend selecting "keep Gmail's copy in the Inbox" so you still have your emails.

6

Optional: Filter specific emails

If you only want to forward certain emails (e.g., from specific senders or with attachments), create a Gmail filter: Settings, then Filters, then Create a new filter. Set your conditions, then choose "Forward it to" your Full Capacity address.

Tip: For the best results, create a Gmail filter that forwards only emails with attachments. This prevents newsletters and other noise from being processed.

Set up auto-forwarding in Outlook / Microsoft 365

1

Open Outlook Settings

Click the gear icon in the top right, then "View all Outlook settings" at the bottom.

2

Go to Mail, then Forwarding

In the left sidebar, click "Mail", then click "Forwarding".

3

Enable forwarding

Check "Enable forwarding". Paste your Full Capacity address in the "Forward my email to" field:

Loading...
4

Keep a copy (recommended)

Check "Keep a copy of forwarded messages" so your Outlook inbox stays intact.

5

Save

Click "Save" at the bottom. Forwarding starts immediately -- no confirmation step needed.

6

Optional: Use Rules for selective forwarding

To forward only specific emails, go to Mail, then Rules, then Add new rule. Set conditions (e.g., "has attachments", "from specific people") and set the action to "Forward to" your Full Capacity address.
